JUDGE · Philosophy · 12 nm

A licence proves you can fly. It doesn’t make you an aviator.

Most training treats you as a transaction — hours sold, boxes ticked, a rating handed over at the end. We think the licence is the easy part. What takes longer is judgement, ethics, and knowing what to do on the day nothing goes to plan.

The usual way

Chasing a licence

  • Hours logged as a number to hit.
  • Answers shaped to close a sale.
  • Theory memorised, then forgotten.
  • You’re a customer until you pass — then you’re on your own.
Our way

Becoming an aviator

  • Hours flown as judgement earned.
  • Honest answers, including the unprofitable ones.
  • Theory you can actually use when it matters.
  • You’re part of the community for good — before and long after the rating.

Real experience over theory. Honest answers over sales pitches. Reality-check mentorship from people who have actually been there.

The true joy of flying is bigger than any title.
